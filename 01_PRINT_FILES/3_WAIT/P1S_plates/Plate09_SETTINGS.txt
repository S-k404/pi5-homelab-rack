09_PLA_screen-module_WAIT-measure-screen-first
==============================================
Status: WAIT: measure screen with calipers upon arrival (arrives 4 Nov)
Material: PLA, about 71 g (or PETG 73 g)

Model Type: Waveshare 7inch 1024×600 HDMI, IPS, Low Power Capacitive LCD (C) Touch Screen
=========================================================================================
This 3U module is specifically modeled for the capacitive touch "C-type" screen
(glass panel with bonded rear driver PCB and 4 corner mounting tabs):

Key Dimensional Comparison:
  - Non-touch panel (legacy v7): 165 x 100 x 5 mm, active 154.08 x 85.92 mm
  - Touch (C) panel (v7.3 Display7C):
      * Board outline (W x H x D): 164.90 x 106.96 x 8.00 mm
      * Height over corner tabs: 124.16 mm (approx 124.2 mm)
      * Glass front to PCB back: 8.00 mm
      * Active display area: 154.21 x 85.92 mm
      * Corner ear tabs: ~5.5 mm wide x 8.5 mm long (with M3 mounting holes)
      * Side connectors: Full-size HDMI + micro-USB touch/power on one short edge

Why Display7C fits the Touch (C) panel:
  - Pocket recess: 165.8 x 124.96 mm (0.4 mm clearance around 164.9 x 124.16 mm outline)
  - Bezel window: 160.0 x 94.0 mm (completely displays the 154.21 x 85.92 mm active area
    with 2.9 mm lateral / 4.0 mm vertical positioning tolerance, while concealing glass edges)
  - Depth recess: 8.0 mm pocket depth + 1.0 mm foam tape under the 4 clamping clips
  - Clamping: 4 corner clips (Display7C_Clip_x4.stl on Plate 04) hold the tabs securely
    using M3 heat-set inserts and M3x8 button head screws.

How to slice & print:
  - Bambu Studio profile: 0.20mm Standard @BBL P1S
  - Filament: Generic PLA, silver like the rest of the PLA parts (Robu: PLA silver, 15 %)
  - Wall loops: 3
  - Sparse infill: 15% gyroid
  - Supports: OFF (bezel prints face down on textured PEI bed)
  - Enclosure: Top lid OPEN

Caliper Checklist (Upon Screen Arrival):
  1. Width across glass / PCB: 164.9 - 165.0 mm
  2. Total vertical height over corner tabs: 124.0 - 124.4 mm
  3. Thickness from front glass to rear PCB surface: 7.8 - 8.2 mm
  4. Tab holes (short slots, Ø3.3): centres 157.0 mm apart across; up/down the slot centres are ~114.9 mm apart
     (112.8 between the slots' inner ends, 117.0 between their outer ends). Corrected 28 Oct from "~159 x ~114":
     measured on Waveshare's STEP of the screen.
  If numbers 1-3 match within ±0.5 mm and the holes are where item 4 says, slice Plate09.3mf and print.
