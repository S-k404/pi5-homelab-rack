Plate 11W (PETG): Argon Poly+ 5 tray + Waveshare adapter (F) holder base + lid  --  WAIT
=====================================================================================
1. The Poly+ 5 tray: measure the case. It takes up to 96.0 long x 66.5 wide (listed 93.5 x 64 x 30, +-1 cm).
   Bigger: don't print, ask Claude to widen the pocket.
2. The holder (base + lid): FIT TEST. The holder sits 3-5 mm from the case's side ports (USB-C power, both
   micro-HDMI), so it only works if the adapter (F)'s own plugs reach the Pi through the Poly+ 5's side cut-outs.
   Test: Pi in the Poly+ 5, lid on; hold the adapter flat beside it, plug edge to the case, push its USB-C and
   micro-HDMI into the cut-outs. Both must click fully home (about 6 mm in) with the adapter level. Also check
   the 16-pin FFC can leave the case. Fails: don't print the holder (delete it from the plate); power the Pi
   from a 12 V -> 5 V USB-C step-down and use the UGREEN micro-HDMI extender (no NVMe / 2.5G then).
Material: PETG black, about 95 g (tray ~43 g, holder base ~35 g, lid ~17 g)

Settings (Bambu P1S): 0.20mm Standard @BBL P1S, Generic PETG, 3 walls, 20% gyroid, supports OFF,
Textured PEI no glue, enclosure CLOSED.
The holder lid is saved TOP FACE DOWN: print it as it is.
