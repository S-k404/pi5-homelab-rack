## Added for the Argon Poly+ 5: not yet in any cart (updated 28 Oct)
The build is **Poly+ 5 + the adapter (F) in its holder, fit test first** (`README.md` in this folder). What you need depends on the test:
- **Always:** a right-angle 5.5 × 2.1 pigtail with bare ends, **at least 30 cm** (UPS OUT → the adapter's screw terminal; Amazon SHOKITECH ≈ ₹151).
- **Always (28 Oct):** a right-angle 5.5 × 2.1 **male → straight female extension, ~30 cm**: UPS DC-IN → the brick's plug, all behind the faceplate.
- **If the fit test passes:** **4 × M2.5 × 25 mm pan-head screws** for the holder. Not searched on Robu yet; check they're pan/button heads (not countersunk). The holder's 2.1 mm pilots let them cut their own thread. The NVMe and the 2.5G port work.
- **Only if the fit test fails:**
  - **12 V → 5 V, 5 A USB-C step-down converter** (UPS OUT → pigtail → converter → Pi USB-C). Robu leads: "24V/12V to 5V 5A Power Module DC-DC XY-3606" (₹139) and "DC-DC Step-Down Buck Converter 24V 12V 9V to 5V 5A 25W" (₹199); neither listing says it has a USB-C output, so check before buying.
  - **UGREEN 20134 micro-HDMI extender** (Amazon only).
  - Then there's no NVMe and no 2.5G port: the microSD above is the boot drive.
