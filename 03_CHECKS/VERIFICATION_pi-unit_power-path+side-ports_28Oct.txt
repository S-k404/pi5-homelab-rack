Pi unit power path (Argon Poly+ 5 build): checks, 28 Oct 2026
Calculations on the CAD models, not a physical test. v3 frame: x left->right, y rack rear(-)->front(+), z up.

== The UPS (Cuzor Mini UPS Pro 12 V 3 A) ==
INFO  size used  100 x 80 x 28 (Cuzor's page: 10 x 8 x 2.8 cm). The tray takes up to 101 x 81 x 28.5. One marketplace listing says 10.2 x 8 x 2.4: if yours is 102 long it is 1 mm too long, so measure it
INFO  switch  Cuzor's page: a SLIDE switch (0 / 1), not a rocker. Its position isn't published
INFO  jacks in the CAD model  one on the rear face at x 86.6, z 11.5 (taken as OUT) and one on the front face at x 14.6 (DC-IN). Cuzor publish no drawing; the v3 case says the jack positions came from product photos
WARN  the CAD model's DC-IN lines up with the UPS tray's front window (x 80..112) and the faceplate window  (model DC-IN at x 14.6: OUTSIDE: behind the solid front wall of the UPS tray). The tray and faceplate windows assume DC-IN at the RIGHT end of the front face (x ~86-106). Check where your UPS's IN jack really is before printing plate 10 / the faceplate

== Right-angle 5.5 x 2.1 plug in the UPS OUT (rack rear) ==
PASS  plug body (11 x 12, 16 deep) passes the UPS tray's rear window (x 58..110, z 5..25)  (body x 81.1..92.1, z 5.5..17.5)
PASS  plug + strain relief (cable out to the right) touch nothing  (worst UPS tray: 0.0 mm3; the plug sticks out 13 mm behind the case's rear face, into the open rack rear: the rear base bar is ~73 mm further back)
WARN  room round the plug body in the window  (17.9 mm each side across, 0.5 mm at the bottom: a 12 mm tall overmould centred on a jack at z 11.5 sits 0.5 mm above the window's bottom edge. If your jack is lower, or the plug fatter, file the window's bottom edge)

== Lead from the plug to the adapter (F)'s screw terminal (in the F holder) ==
INFO  route  OUT plug (rear) -> right channel x 101.5..113.9 -> front window -> pocket behind the faceplate, under the tray -> lead hole (14..26, 96..104) -> holder front notch (x 28..42) -> terminal. Length about 26 cm from the plug; pigtails are usually 15-30 cm
PASS  a 4.0 mm lead along that route touches nothing  (worst UPS: 0.0 mm3)
WARN  a 5.5 mm lead along that route touches nothing  (worst holder base: 14.3 mm3)
INFO  pinch in the right channel  the M3 joint post at x 107.7, y 40.0 (dia 6.5, full height) leaves 2.95 / 2.95 mm either side in the 12.4 mm channel, too narrow for a 4 mm lead. The route lifts the lead over the UPS's top edge there, through the 5.5 mm air gap between the UPS and the tray floor
PASS  the lead clears the brick's plug in DC-IN (dia 11, assumed at the window centre x 96, z 17)  (0.0 mm3)
PASS  the tray's lead hole (12 x 8) takes two 4 mm leads side by side (one to the terminal, one to a 12 V -> 5 V converter if you also use one)  
INFO  terminal access  the terminal block's wire entries face the front notch (y 95.8); its screws are on top: lid off, strip ~7 mm, screw down, lid on. The ~13 mm strip in front of the holder (y 98..112) is also where the Argon plan puts the 12 V -> 5 V converter, so if you fit both, run this lead over the converter

== The Pi's side ports (USB-C power, 2 x micro-HDMI) with the F holder beside the case ==
FAIL  a USB-C / micro-HDMI plug (32 mm with its strain relief) fits into each side port, case nominal 64 mm  (holder is 5.2 mm from the case's side; USB-C power y 76: 1121 mm3 of holder in the way, micro-HDMI 0 y 62: 814 mm3 of holder in the way, micro-HDMI 1 y 48: 737 mm3 of holder in the way)
FAIL  a USB-C / micro-HDMI plug (32 mm with its strain relief) fits into each side port, case max 66 mm  (holder is 3.2 mm from the case's side; USB-C power y 76: 1173 mm3 of holder in the way, micro-HDMI 0 y 62: 852 mm3 of holder in the way, micro-HDMI 1 y 48: 761 mm3 of holder in the way)
INFO  what this means  The holder puts the adapter (F)'s own USB-C and micro-HDMI plugs in line with the Pi's side ports, ~1.2 mm short of the case wall. So EITHER the adapter's plugs reach the Pi through the Poly+ 5's side cut-outs (then the Pi is powered and drives HDMI through the adapter, fed by this UPS OUT -> terminal lead; no step-down converter or UGREEN needed), OR they don't, and then nothing else can plug into those ports either: the step-down converter's USB-C and the UGREEN micro-HDMI extender from the Argon plan have no room while the holder is there. Measure the Poly+ 5's side cut-outs (wall thickness, how far the Pi's USB-C sits behind the outside face) with the adapter in hand before printing the holder.
