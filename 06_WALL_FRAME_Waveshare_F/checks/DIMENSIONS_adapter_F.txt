Waveshare Pi5 Connector Adapter (F): dimensions checked, 8 Nov 2026
====================================================================
Sources
  A  Waveshare's dimension drawing ("Pi5-Connector-Adapter-F-details-size", 960 x 555 px, the same file on
     docs.waveshare.com and waveshare.com). Printed figures: 85.00 / 86.00 long, 52.50 wide, holes 58.00 x 46.50,
     49.50 (top edge to the far hole row).
  B  waveshare.com product page: 86.0 x 52.5 mm, 47 g, 2230 / 2242 SSDs, 16-pin FFC 100 mm, SH1.0 3-pin leads.
  C  Raspberry Pi 5 mechanical drawing (datasheets.raspberrypi.com): USB-C at 11.2, micro-HDMI 0 at 25.8 and 1 at 39.2
     from the board end, holes 3.5 in, 58 x 49, Ø2.7.
  D  Pixel analysis of A (scripts measure_*.py): edges, hole rings and parts found by colour, positions taken
     relative to the four hole centres, because the holes are what locate the board in the frame.

Scale found in A: 6.775 px/mm along (hole rings 392.9 px = 58.00), 6.817 px/mm across (317 px = 46.50). The photo
is not a true-scale drawing: the board measures 85.24 long against the printed 85.00, and the 46.50 / 49.50 arrows
end at the board edge rather than at the hole centre. Treat everything below as good to about +-0.3 mm.

Cross-check C: when the adapter is plugged into a Pi 5, its plugs sit on the Pi's USB-C and micro-HDMI 0, 14.6 apart.
D measures the plug centres 14.2 apart; C puts them at 59.75 / 74.35, D at 60.2 / 74.4: the same to within 0.45 mm.

What changed in the frame after this check: right inner wall +0.7 (terminal end), bottom and top margins +0.2
(plug tips), HDMI and rod positions; outline 103.6 x 72.1 -> 104.3 x 72.5. Keyholes still 28.0 apart.

Not measurable from any source: heights (the drawing is a top view). RJ45 13.5 and HDMI 6.5 come from typical parts;
the frame leaves 15.0 mm above the board.
