Every cable in the 28 Oct build: plugs, routes, clearances and lengths (ultrareview, 28 Oct 2026)
Calculations on the CAD models and stand-ins, not a physical test. Rack frame: x across (+x = viewer's LEFT from the front), y up from the base-bar tops, z back from the front rails, mm.
Cables are routed up the open rack rear on the -x side (the viewer's RIGHT seen from the front; the 12 V brick and its mains cord are on the other side). Lengths are port face to port face along the route + 10 %.

== 12 V brick lead: route length and the spare lead ==
INFO  brick lead used  (about 204 mm from the brick to the DC-IN, plug included)
WARN  the spare lead has to go somewhere  (Robu doesn't list the lead length; bricks like it come with 1.0 to 1.5 m, so ~796 to ~1296 mm is left over. The only room inside is on top of the brick: 110 long x 38 high x ~40 wide under the B1 dock. Coil it in a 100 mm figure-8, zip-tie it and lay it on the brick, or coil it behind the rack. Don't coil it round the brick's body (heat)
