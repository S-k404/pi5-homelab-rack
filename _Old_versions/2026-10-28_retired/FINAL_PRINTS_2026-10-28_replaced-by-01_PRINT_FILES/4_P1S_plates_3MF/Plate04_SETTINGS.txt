04_PLA_3U-module+ears+clips
===========================
Status: print now
Material: PLA, about 128 g (144 g was with the old v4 B1 spacers, which are gone)

How: double-click the .3mf (or File > Open Project in Bambu Studio). Every part is already placed.
If Bambu Studio asks, choose to load the geometry only. Then pick the settings below and Slice.
(The loose STLs are here too, in case you'd rather import them yourself.)

Settings:
  - Bambu Studio profile: 0.24mm Draft @BBL P1S
  - Filament: Generic PLA
  - Wall loops: 3 (default is 2)
  - Sparse infill: 15% gyroid
  - Supports: OFF
  - Plate: Textured PEI
  - Enclosure: top lid or front door OPEN (PLA heat-creeps in a closed P1S)

On this plate:
  - Side 3U x1   (Rack_SideModule_3U_x4.stl)
  - Ear x2   (B1_Dock_3U_Ear_x2.stl)
  - Clip x4   (Display7C_Clip_x4.stl)
