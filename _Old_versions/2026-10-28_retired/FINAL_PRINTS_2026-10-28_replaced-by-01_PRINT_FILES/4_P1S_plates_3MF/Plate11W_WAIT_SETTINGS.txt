Status: WAIT for two things:
  1. The Poly+ 5 tray: measure the case. It takes up to 96.0 long x 66.5 wide (listed 93.5 x 64 x 30, +-1 cm).
     Bigger: don't print, ask Claude to widen the pocket.
  2. The holder (base + lid): the TEST FIT below. The holder sits 3-5 mm from the case's side ports (USB-C power,
     both micro-HDMI). It only works if the adapter (F)'s own USB-C and micro-HDMI plugs reach the Pi through the

     extender instead (no NVMe / 2.5G then).

     its USB-C and micro-HDMI plugs into the cut-outs. Both must click fully home (about 6 mm in) with the adapter
     level. Also check the 16-pin FFC can leave the case and reach the adapter.
