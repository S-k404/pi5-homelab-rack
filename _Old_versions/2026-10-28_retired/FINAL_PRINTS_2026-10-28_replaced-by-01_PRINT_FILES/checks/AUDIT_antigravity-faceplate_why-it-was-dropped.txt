Antigravity "final" faceplate (Rack_Faceplate_2U_10in_final) + UPS switch extender: audit, 28 Oct 2026
Measured on the STEP files in the Pi unit's v3 frame (x left->right, y rack rear -> front, z up; faceplate rear face y 112.5).

Findings:
1. The UPS switch is NOT behind the faceplate. In the v3 case (case_v3.py: UPS_X "switch/LED/OUT end at the right";
   the window x 58..110 z 5..25 is on the y = -2.2 face) the switch, LED and OUT face the RACK REAR. The face of the UPS
   towards the faceplate (y 80.6) carries only DC-IN. Antigravity's renders moved the UPS mesh 29.9 mm forward
   (apply_final_fix_and_render_all.py) to make the switch line up; the printed UPS tray can't hold it there.
2. Behind the new 16 x 11 switch window there is the UPS tray's solid back wall (y 87.0..89.2, 132 mm3 in a 10 x 6
   corridor below). The 38.4 mm extender can't pass it and there is no switch to reach.
3. The "hidden cable slit" removed only 155 mm3: a 9 x 7 mm, 2.5 mm deep groove at z 31..38. The rest of it (z 4..31) and
   the "transit notch" fall inside the existing open DC-IN window, so they cut nothing. The UPS OUT -> terminal lead
   already ran inside the case in v3; nothing changed for it.
4. The notch for plate 13's 12 V brick lead is gone (261 mm3 of material back in it). The brick-cradle check shows the
   faceplate without the notch blocks the lead's route. So with plate 13 the lead had no way out.
Fix: the final set uses Rack_Faceplate_2U_10in_DC-notch (checked: 42 PASS brick, 38 PASS rack, rebuilt byte-identical).
The switch extender (plate 14) is dropped.
