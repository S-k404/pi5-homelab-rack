12_PETG_UCG-Ultra-shelf
=======================
Status: print now
Material: PETG, about 95 g

How: double-click the .3mf (or File > Open Project in Bambu Studio). Every part is already placed.
If Bambu Studio asks, choose to load the geometry only. Then pick the settings below and Slice.
(The loose STLs are here too, in case you'd rather import them yourself.)

Settings:
  - Bambu Studio profile: 0.20mm Standard @BBL P1S
  - Filament: Generic PETG
  - Wall loops: 3
  - Sparse infill: 20% gyroid
  - Supports: OFF
  - Plate: Textured PEI, no glue
  - Enclosure: CLOSED

Notes:
  Sparse infill: 15% gyroid for this part.
  The shelf stands 137 mm tall on its front face. Clean the plate with dish soap first and watch the first layers.
  It is left turned 45 degrees on purpose: that leaves 22 mm round it, so you can add a 5 mm brim if your plate is worn
  (laid straight it would be 254 mm wide with no room for a brim).

On this plate:
  - UCG-Ultra shelf x1   (UCG_Ultra_1U_Shelf.stl)
