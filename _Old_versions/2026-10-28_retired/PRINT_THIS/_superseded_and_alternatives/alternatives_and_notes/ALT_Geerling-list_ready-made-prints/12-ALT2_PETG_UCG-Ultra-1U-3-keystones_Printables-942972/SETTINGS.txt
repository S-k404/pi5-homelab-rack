========================================================
Status: optional. Print this INSTEAD OF plate 12 if you want the ready-made mount (checked against your rack 27 Oct)
Material: PETG, about 120 g
Design: "Unifi Cloud Gateway Ultra 10" rackmount", Printables 942972
        https://www.printables.com/model/942972-unifi-cloud-gateway-ultra-10-rackmount
        For your own printing only: don't re-share the file.

How: double-click the .3mf (or File > Open Project in Bambu Studio). The part is already placed.
If Bambu Studio asks, choose to load the geometry only. Then pick the settings below and Slice.
(The loose STL is here too, in case you'd rather import it yourself.)

Settings:
  - Bambu Studio profile: 0.20mm Standard @BBL P1S
  - Filament: Generic PETG
  - Wall loops: 3
  - Sparse infill: 20% gyroid
  - Supports: OFF
  - Plate: Textured PEI, no glue
  - Enclosure: CLOSED

Notes:
  Changed from the download (the original is untouched in ../_downloads/):
  - 0.8 mm taken off the top edge. As downloaded it's a full 44.45 mm tall and touches your rack's cap (0.00 mm gap).
    Now it's a standard 43.65 mm 1U panel with 0.8 mm of air under the cap. The ear slots haven't moved.
  - Two zero-thickness junk surfaces removed. The 3 keystone holders were separate pieces sitting in the panel
    openings; they're now one part with the panel.
  It prints panel face down and stands 136 mm tall (like plate 12). Clean the plate with dish soap first.
  It's turned 45 degrees on purpose: that leaves 24 mm all round, so you can add a 5 mm brim if your plate is worn.
  Supports OFF: the only overhang is the rear stop at the very top of the print (a 3 mm lip and two corner posts,
  10-14 mm out). Its underside comes out a bit rough. It faces the gateway's back, so it doesn't matter. If you want
  it clean, paint supports on just those faces.
  The 3 keystone slots take standard keystone jacks or blanks (not on the BOM). Without them they're open holes.
  Hardware: 4 x M5 x 12 button head + washers into the rail inserts, the same as plate 12.
  BEFORE RACKING: put the gateway in and plug in its rear cables. A 10 mm lip runs across the back, 2 mm behind
  the gateway, and a corner post covers the last 14 mm of its back on the right (seen from the front). Check the
  RJ45 and USB-C plugs clear them. If one doesn't, ask Claude to cut that part down.
