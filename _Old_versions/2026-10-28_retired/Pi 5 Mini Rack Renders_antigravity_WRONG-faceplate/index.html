Pi 5 Mini Rack

The printed 10-inch, 9U rack in the RackMate T0 style: silver sides, a black vented cap, and four units from the bottom up. Those are the Pi 5 + UPS, the NIIMBOT B1 dock, the 7-inch touch screen and the UniFi UCG-Ultra. Tap any image to see it full size.

Path-traced in Blender 5.2 (Cycles, Metal GPU) from the CadQuery sources in rework-01, with the same part positions as the interactive 3D page. The Pi 5 and the 7-inch screen use the makers' 3D models. The B1, UCG-Ultra, UPS and cooler are simplified shapes at their real sizes. The screen and gateway displays show sample images.

Full-size PNGs and the Blender scene (Pi5_MiniRack_v7.2_studio.blend, one camera per view) are in VISUALS/blender/.
