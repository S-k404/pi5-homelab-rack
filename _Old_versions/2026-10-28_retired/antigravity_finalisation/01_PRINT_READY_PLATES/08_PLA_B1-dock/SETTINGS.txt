08_PLA_B1-dock
==============
Status: print now (dock v5: the real B1, 132 x 91 x 62)
Material: PLA, about 120 g

How: double-click the .3mf (or File > Open Project in Bambu Studio). Every part is already placed.
If Bambu Studio asks, choose to load the geometry only. Then pick the settings below and Slice.
(The loose STLs are here too, in case you'd rather import them yourself.)

Settings:
  - Bambu Studio profile: 0.20mm Standard @BBL P1S
  - Filament: Generic PLA
  - Wall loops: 3
  - Sparse infill: 15% gyroid
  - Supports: OFF
  - Plate: Textured PEI
  - Enclosure: top lid OPEN

Notes:
  Dock v5 (27 Oct): sized to the B1's official 132 x 91 x 62 mm (NIIMBOT manual) and laid out from your photos.
  No spacers any more. Stand the B1 face out with its open slide on TOP: the lid end is then on your right,
  and the lid can swing open in the rack to change the label roll.

On this plate:
  - B1 dock body x1   (B1_Dock_3U_Body.stl)
