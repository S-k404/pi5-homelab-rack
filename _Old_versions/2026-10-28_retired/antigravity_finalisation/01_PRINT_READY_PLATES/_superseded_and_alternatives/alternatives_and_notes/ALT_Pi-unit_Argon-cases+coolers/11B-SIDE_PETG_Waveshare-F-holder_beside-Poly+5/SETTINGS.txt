11B-SIDE_PETG_Waveshare-F-holder_beside-Poly+5
==============================================
Status: optional add-on to plate 11B (the Argon Poly+5 build). Print it only if you want the Waveshare adapter (F)
        (NVMe + 2.5G Ethernet) to live beside the Poly+5. Read README.md in this folder first.
Material: PETG, black. About 19 g (base) + 12 g (lid) at 20% infill.

Print BOTH parts. Each STL is already saved the way it prints:
  - Waveshare_F_holder_base.stl   x1   plinth down (it is 56 x 102.6 x 25.8 mm)
  - Waveshare_F_holder_lid.stl    x1   TOP FACE DOWN (grooves on the bed, the 4 pillars point up; 56 x 102.6 x 17.3 mm)
Both fit on one 256 mm bed side by side if you want a single plate (56 + 56 mm wide, 102.6 mm long).

Settings:
  - Bambu Studio profile: 0.20mm Standard @BBL P1S
  - Filament: Generic PETG
  - Wall loops: 3
  - Sparse infill: 20% gyroid
  - Supports: OFF (the longest bridge is 5.6 mm: the deck over the plinth ribs)
  - Plate: Textured PEI, no glue
  - Enclosure: CLOSED
  - Brim: off

Hardware:
  - 4 x M2.5 x 25 pan-head screws. They hold the lid AND clamp the board's four mounting holes to the posts.
    They cut their own thread in the 2.1 mm pilots (like the Pi screws in the v3 tray). Replaces the 4 x M2.5 x 10 +
    shims that the bare-board v3 case used for the adapter.
  - Double-sided foam tape under the right-hand rib (already on the BOM), to stop the holder sliding on the tray.
  - Adapter (F) with its 16-pin FFC cable, the SSD (M.2 2230 or 2242), the 12 V lead if you use the terminal.

On this plate:
  - Holder base x1   (Waveshare_F_holder_base.stl)
  - Holder lid x1    (Waveshare_F_holder_lid.stl)
