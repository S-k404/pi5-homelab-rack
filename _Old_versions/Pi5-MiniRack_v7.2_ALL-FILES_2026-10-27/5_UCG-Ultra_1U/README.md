# UniFi UCG-Ultra shelf — 10-inch 1U (v7.2, top of the 9U rack)

One print holds a UCG-Ultra (141.8 × 127.6 × 30 mm, 0.52 kg) at the top of the rack, just under the cap. The front panel has the ears built in, and a window shows the gateway's front face and display. The tray has 12 mm side rails, floor vents and two rear corner stops. The ports and USB-C power at the back stay clear.

- **Print:** `STL_for_printing/UCG_Ultra_1U_Shelf.stl`, front face down. It's already turned 45° on the bed, which needs 211 mm; on the P1S it also fits straight. It's **137 mm tall**, needs no supports, and takes about 95 g. **Use PETG**, because the gateway runs warm.
- **Fit:** drop the gateway into the tray, display forward, then rack the shelf with 4 × M5 × 12 screws and washers into the rail inserts at the top U.
- **Cables:** plug them in before you rack the shelf, or steady the rack while you plug in. About 5 N on the gateway's back tips the rack forward, and an RJ45 plug can need 20 N.
- **Check:** if a plug touches a corner stop on your gateway, trim that stop.
- **Checks:** `VERIFICATION_ucg.txt` has 15 PASS. The rack check (`1_Rack_9U_T0-look/VERIFICATION_rack.txt`) has 39 PASS, 0 FAIL, with the shelf in place. These are model calculations, not a physical test.
